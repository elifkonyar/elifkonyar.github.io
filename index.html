Elif Konyar

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Systems Engineering at The University of Texas at Dallas.

My research focuses on developing human-centered analytics for complex systems, at the intersection of data science, optimization, and systems thinking. Motivated by the challenges of understanding human states, needs, and behaviors in complex environments, I develop interpretable, robust, and privacy-preserving methods that transform high-dimensional, multimodal, and distributed data into trustworthy insights. My research explores advanced data modeling, multimodal data fusion, and privacy-preserving analytics, drawing on diverse data sources, including electronic health records, wearables, and human interactions with intelligent systems. My work spans healthcare and transportation, as well as human-in-the-loop settings involving immersive technologies and human-AI collaboration.

I was a Carmine and Jean Iannacone Postdoctoral Fellow working with Dr. Kamran Paynabar in the School of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISyE) at Georgia Institute of Technology. I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial & Systems Engineering at the University of Florida under the supervision of Dr. Mostafa Reisi Gahrooei.
